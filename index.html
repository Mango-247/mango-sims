Mango Sims Uploader

A personal tool for the Mango Sims channel.

Mango Sims posts short physics and ball simulation videos. The videos are rendered with code, and this tool uploads one finished video a day to the Mango Sims YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own channel. It is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's account.

Channel: youtube.com/@verycoolmango
